🗝️ First time at the castle door? Start here ▶
1

The door knows you by email

Use the email address the krewe has on the member roster. Not sure which one that is? Ask at digital@kreweofshamrock.com.

2

Forge your key (set a password)

Use the gold button below with the email on the member roster. If you are on the list, we email a first-login link. If that address is not on the roster, the door will say so. Then click the link on this device and choose a password of at least 8 characters.

3

Open the door

Come back here and sign in with your email and new password. Your browser can remember it for next time. Officers, board members, and committee chairs confirm their directory titles (President, Board Member, Committee Chair of…) on the first-login questionnaire so the right desk tools unlock.

What’s waiting inside: a quick Craic Cup welcome (photo, name, a couple playful prompts) gets you into the game. Title claims (officer, board, committee chair) stay optional and match the Shamrock Leaders directory. Then it’s your personal hub — your standing and next actions on Home, plus tabs for My Krewe, Events, Parade Day, Give Back, and Fun. Officers see an extra Officer desk. Role requests are confirmed by an officer, usually within a day — the rest of the castle is yours right away.

Stuck at the door? Email digital@kreweofshamrock.com and a human will let you in.
