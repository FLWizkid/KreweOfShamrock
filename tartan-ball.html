Join us for an evening of tradition, celebration, and distinction as the Krewe of Shamrock honors the ceremonial crowning of our new King & Queen. Expect candlelight and chandeliers, the skirl of the pipes, and a night of royal Irish splendor with the finest company in Tampa Bay.

The Evening’s Program

How the night unfolds

6:00 – 7:00 PM — Cocktail hour with pictures and the Moon Coin band

7:00 – 8:00 PM — Dinner with presentations

8:00 – 10:00 PM — Dancing, 50/50 purchases, and raffle ticket purchasing

10:00 PM — Bar closes; the 50/50, raffles, and liquor cart are drawn and awarded

An Evening of Dancing & Finery

The Tartan Ball is our most elegant night of the year: a grand ballroom, a turn around the floor, and the warmth of old friends. Think formal attire with a Celtic touch: a sash, a tartan, a kilt, or your sharpest black-tie green.

From the first waltz to the last reel, it’s a night to dress up, dance, and celebrate together.

Sponsor the Ball

Put your name — or your company’s — behind the season’s grandest evening. Sponsor the Moon Coin band, the photography, the Irish dancers, or chip in $50 toward the liquor wagon. Sponsors appear on event signage, are thanked during the 7:00 PM dinner presentations, and get a social media shoutout.

The Krewe of Shamrock is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, so your payment receipt doubles as your tax receipt.
